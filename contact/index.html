Frequently asked questions

What are the room prices at Shri Maha Maya Hotel & Restaurant?

Standard AC rooms are ₹1,000, Deluxe AC ₹1,200 and Family AC ₹1,400 per room per night. Prices are per room, not per person.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is possible any time — our front desk is open 24×7. Check-out is at 11:00 AM.

Is the restaurant really 100% pure vegetarian?

Yes. Our kitchen is completely pure vegetarian with no eggs. Jain food can be prepared on request.

Do you have parking for buses and trucks?

Yes. There is ample parking right off the highway for cars, SUVs, buses and trucks, beside the Indian Oil fuel station.

Is Wi-Fi available at the hotel?

Yes, free Wi-Fi is available in rooms, the lobby and the restaurant.

Is the front desk open at night?

Yes, the front desk and room service run 24×7. The kitchen serves from 6:00 AM to 12:30 AM.

Where exactly is the hotel located?

We are on the main highway at Pallu, Hanumangarh, Rajasthan 335522, beside the Indian Oil fuel station.

How do I book a room?

Call or WhatsApp us on +91 81073 01306, or send the booking form on this website. An advance of half the room rent or more confirms your booking.

Which cities and routes do travellers come from?

Guests stop with us while travelling between Pallu, Hanumangarh, Sri Ganganagar, Nohar, Bikaner, Bathinda and Sirsa on the highway.

Do you have family rooms?

Yes, the Family AC room sleeps 3–4 guests at ₹1,400 per night. An extra bed can be added for ₹200.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash and UPI payments.

Are pets allowed at the hotel?

Yes, pets are allowed at the hotel.
