Room types and rates

Shri Maha Maya Hotel & Restaurant has ten air-conditioned guest rooms in three types. Every rate is per room per night, not per person, so a couple pays the same as a solo traveller. Booking is simple: call or WhatsApp us, pay an advance of half the room rent or more, and your room is held for you.

Standard AC Room — ₹1,000 per night

Simple, spotless and easy on the pocket. A comfortable queen bed, working AC, attached bathroom with geyser and room service on call. Sleeps 2–3 guests — the right pick for solo travellers, couples and pilgrims who want a good night without extra frills.

Deluxe AC Room — ₹1,200 per night

A little more space and light, with a private balcony to step out onto after a long drive. Same queen bed, attached bath, geyser and 24×7 room service. Sleeps 2–3 guests — couples on a road trip usually pick this one.

Check Availability →

Family AC Room — ₹1,400 per night

Our largest room, made for families: two beds, a table and chairs, and space for 3–4 guests. An extra bed can be added for ₹200 per night. Attached bathroom, geyser, AC and round-the-clock room service included.

What every room includes

  • Air conditioning and a comfortable bed with clean linen
  • Attached bathroom with geyser (hot water at any hour)
  • 24×7 room service from our pure-veg kitchen
  • Free Wi-Fi in rooms, lobby and restaurant
  • CCTV-monitored premises and secure parking beside the hotel
  • Balcony in Deluxe; table-chairs and two beds in Family

Check-in, payment and house rules

Check-in is possible at any time because the front desk never closes; check-out is at 11:00 AM. Please carry a government photo ID (Aadhaar, driving licence or voter ID) for every booking. An advance of half the room rent or more confirms your reservation; cancellations are fully refunded only within 20–30 minutes of booking.

We accept cash and UPI. Pets are welcome at the hotel. Please note we do not provide driver or staff accommodation, and breakfast is not included in room rates — though the kitchen is always a short call away.

Which room should you pick?

Travelling solo or as a couple on a budget, the Standard AC room covers everything you need. Want a balcony and a little more room to breathe after hours on the highway, choose Deluxe. With children or a small group of three to four, the Family AC room with two beds and a sitting table is the comfortable choice — and the pure-veg thali is never more than a phone call away.
