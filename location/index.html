A natural halt on the highway: fuel up next door, eat a hot thali across the counter, and sleep in a cool, quiet room a few steps away.

Exact address and landmark

Shri Maha Maya Hotel & Restaurant stands on the main highway at Pallu, Hanumangarh district, Rajasthan 335522, directly beside the Indian Oil fuel station. If you can see the fuel station, you have already seen our signboard. Ample parking for cars, SUVs, buses and trucks sits right off the highway.

Serving travellers from

Travellers across this belt of Rajasthan make Shri Maha Maya their regular halt. Approximate road distances from the hotel:

  • Rawatsar — approx. 40 km
  • Sardarshahar — approx. 60 km
  • Nohar — approx. 70 km
  • Hanumangarh — approx. 80 km

On the highway route

The hotel sits on the busy highway corridor that links Bikaner and Hanumangarh towards the Sri Ganganagar side, with traffic from the Bathinda and Sirsa direction joining the same stretch. That makes it an easy, well-lit stop: no narrow lanes, no U-turns — turn in beside the fuel station and you are parked.

Use the map below for turn-by-turn navigation, open our Google Maps listing to save the place, or read and write reviews on our Google profile. Coordinates for GPS devices: 28.938974, 74.216543.

Parking, fuel and traveller basics

Fuel next door, ample parking beside the hotel, clean washrooms and a pure-veg kitchen open till 12:30 AM: the small things that matter on a long drive are all handled in one stop. Bus and truck crews can park overnight beside the hotel and request 5 AM chai before an early departure.
